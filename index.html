SDG-E • COMPONENTS

Parts Used

The components and hardware used to build our SDG-E robot.

01

Three Micro:bits

We used three Micro:bits, with each one having a different role in the robot's system.

02

Micro:bit — Soil Moisture Sensor

The first Micro:bit is connected to the soil moisture sensor (SMS). It is responsible for reading the moisture level of the soil.

When the soil is detected as WET, the reading can be sent to the rest of the system.

03

Micro:bit — Extra Functions

The second Micro:bit handles additional functions of the robot.

  • Battery system
  • Cool animations
  • Speaker
  • Radio broadcasting
04

Micro:bit — WET Indicator

The third Micro:bit receives the WET reading from the first Micro:bit.

When the soil is well moisturized, this Micro:bit lights up its own LED to indicate that the soil has sufficient moisture.

05

Avishkaar Microcontroller

We used an Avishkaar microcontroller for one of the robot's main mechanical functions: controlling the hatch.

Its main purpose is to operate the mechanism that opens the hatch.

06

Avishkaar Metal Parts

We used metal construction parts from Avishkaar to build the structure of the robot.

The metal parts were later given a paint job to match the appearance and design of our robot.

07

Avishkaar Ultrasonic Sensor

An Avishkaar ultrasonic sensor is positioned as the head of the robot.

We chose this placement because its appearance resembles the head of WALL-E, which fits the design we had previously planned for the robot.

08

Strong DC Motors

We used strong DC motors to power the two tires underneath the robot.

One motor powers one side of the robot, while another motor powers the other side, allowing the robot to move using its two driven wheels.

09

Power Switches

Switches were placed at the back of the robot to control power to the two strong DC motors.

This gives us a convenient way to control the motor system from the rear of the robot.
